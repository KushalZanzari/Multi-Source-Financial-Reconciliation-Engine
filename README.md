## 🎯 Intended User, Bottleneck, & Why It Matters

### Intended User
Finance and Operations teams at e-commerce merchants, marketplace aggregators, and fintech companies responsible for daily payment reconciliation across bank accounts, payment gateway settlement reports (Payment Gateway, Stripe, PayU), and internal ERP sales ledgers.

### The Bottleneck
Manual financial reconciliation is notoriously slow, error-prone, and painful. Finance teams spend **45+ minutes per 10 records** manually cross-referencing order IDs, deducting gateway fees (2% + 18% GST), catching single-character OCR typos, matching 3-day bank settlement windows, and unbundling many-to-one payout deposits.

### Why It Matters
Un-reconciled transactions lead to revenue leakage, duplicate refund payouts, undetected gateway fee overcharges, and audit failures. Automated AI reconciliation eliminates **84.4% of manual ops time** while maintaining human-in-the-loop oversight on ambiguous or high-risk exceptions.

## 🛡️ Ground Rules Compliance

1. **Synthetic Data Only**: All evaluation cases in `eval/eval_cases.json` and datasets in `data/` are 100% synthetically generated. No real corporate or customer data is included.
2. **Approval-Before-Action Checkpoint**: High-risk or low-confidence matches (<70% confidence) are automatically enqueued into the Human Review Queue (`src/review.py`). Auto-matches are strictly gated before any downstream financial settlement.
3. **No Secrets Committed**: API keys and environment configurations are loaded strictly via `.env` using `python-dotenv`. `.env.example` is provided; zero credentials exist in the repository.
